Claude Code v2.1.133 (May 7)

Released May 7, 23:49 UTC. New `worktree.baseRef` setting (`fresh` | `head`) — choose whether --worktree, EnterWorktree, and agent-isolation worktrees branch from `origin/<default>` or local HEAD, critical for repos with unpushed commits. Hooks now receive the active effort level via `effort.level` JSON field and `$CLAUDE_EFFORT` env var, enabling effort-gated automation. Critical fixes: parallel sessions losing 401 auth after token-refresh race conditions; subagents failing to discover project/user/plugin skills; subagents in isolated worktrees denied read/edit access to their own files; stale worktrees from prior sessions being reused; `HTTP(S)_PROXY` and mTLS not respected for full MCP OAuth flow; `Edit`/`Write` allow rules matching incorrectly on drive roots.
