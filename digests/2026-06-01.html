Claude Code Billing Split — June 15, 2026

On June 15 — 14 days away — Anthropic splits Claude subscription usage into two pools: interactive Claude Code in the terminal and IDE keeps drawing from your plan limits unchanged, while programmatic use (Agent SDK, `claude -p`, GitHub Actions integrations, third-party agents) moves to a separate monthly credit: $20 for Pro, $100 for Max 5x, $200 for Max 20x, billed at full API list rates with no rollover. Two actions required before June 15: (1) claim your credit via the email Anthropic is sending, (2) decide whether to enable 'usage credits' so overflow bills rather than fails. 8+ detailed community guides published this week; active discussion across every Claude community.
