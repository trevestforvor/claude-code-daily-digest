Anthropic Files S-1 — Then Claude Goes Down

Anthropic confidentially filed its S-1 with the SEC on June 1, reporting $47B annualized revenue and a $965B valuation — then Claude went down globally on June 2 from ~0600–1042 UTC. Root cause: a malfunction in Claude Code's sub-agent system caused agents to multiply in a loop, driving unexpected token consumption spikes. Quotas were reset for all affected users.
