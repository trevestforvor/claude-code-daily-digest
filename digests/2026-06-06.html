Claude Code v2.1.166 (June 6) — fallbackModel, Thinking Disabled, Deny-Rule Globs

Today's release is the largest Claude Code changelog in weeks. Three headline additions: (1) `fallbackModel` settings field — configure up to three fallback models tried in order when your primary is overloaded or unavailable, `--fallback-model` now applies to interactive sessions and retries once on the fallback when the API returns unexpected non-retryable errors; (2) Thinking control — `MAX_THINKING_TOKENS=0`, `--thinking disabled`, and per-model toggles now fully disable thinking on models that reason by default; (3) Glob patterns in deny rules — `"*"` in a deny rule's tool-name slot blocks all tools, enabling blanket sandboxing. Security fix: `SendMessage` relays from other Claude sessions no longer carry user authority, closing an elevation path in multi-agent setups.

Claude Outage June 5 + Anthropic Investigates Potential Cross-User Data Exposure

Anthropic's Claude platform suffered a roughly 5h45m outage Friday June 5 (8:08–18:27 UTC) — the third major disruption this week after elevated errors on June 1 and the June 2 sub-agent loop that triggered the IPO-day outage. More serious: Anthropic announced it is investigating social media reports that some users received responses intended for other users during the disruption. As of Friday afternoon the company had not confirmed any data exposure but called the reports 'serious' and is actively investigating. Claude Code, Claude.ai, and Cowork were all affected; quotas were reset for impacted users.
