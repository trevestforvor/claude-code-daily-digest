Graphify

Parses code, docs, PDFs, images, and video locally with tree-sitter AST into a queryable knowledge graph — no embeddings, no vector store. Each connection is tagged EXTRACTED (explicit) or INFERRED (resolved). Installs a `/graphify` skill and registers hooks that redirect file-search tool calls to the graph. `uv tool install graphifyy && graphify install` auto-detects your agent; `graphify claude install` adds per-project setup. Output: graph.html, GRAPH_REPORT.md, graph.json. 140k stars, 13.3k forks.
