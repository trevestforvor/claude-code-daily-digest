Planning with Files

28.1k stars, Manus-style file-based planning for long-running agents. Keeps three markdown files in the project (task_plan.md, findings.md, progress.md) and uses lifecycle hooks to re-inject the plan every turn, so it survives /clear, context compaction, and crashes. A gated mode holds the agent from stopping mid-phase; SHA-256 attestation refuses to inject a plan whose contents were modified outside the session. Direct cost benefit: persistent context means no token-burning re-discovery after a session reset.
