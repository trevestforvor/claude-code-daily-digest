reanimate

New skill that reverse-engineers any animation — live URL, MP4, GIF, screen recording, or Lottie — and rebuilds it in your stack. The 5-step loop records the original, writes a per-element timing-and-easing spec, builds with CSS/Framer Motion/GSAP/three.js, compares frame-by-frame (reports pixel mismatch and diff images), and repeats until match. Add `--gl` for WebGL/WebGPU/Canvas capture including shaders and three.js scene graphs.
