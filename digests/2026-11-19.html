Skills → Plugins: Enterprise Cutover Nov 30

On November 30, Anthropic renames "Skills" to "Plugins" on Enterprise plans (Team and other plans began Oct 29). Critical admin action: if Skills are currently **off** for your org, plugins will also stop working in Claude Desktop after Nov 30. To keep plugins running: Organization settings → Library → Policy → enable "Cloud code execution" and turn on Skills before cutover. No action needed if Skills are already on.
